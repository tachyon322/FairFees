## What this actually is

On pons v2, every trade can carry two charges:

1. The **standard trading fee** — split between pons, the creator, and an optional buyback.
2. An optional **creator tax** — set at launch, capped by the protocol, fixed forever, paid entirely to the creator fee recipient.

Most tokens use that second charge as a founder salary. Fair Fees uses it as a holder split.

`$FEES` launches with a **3% creator tax**. The `creatorFeeRecipient` is not a person. It is a contract. That contract claims the accrued fees and distributes them to holders every 60 seconds, proportional to each wallet’s share of supply.

If you hold 1% of supply, you receive 1% of the fees.

The payout is the quote asset of the launch — **ETH**, if the pair is ETH. Not a paper drip of the token itself. Volume prints, holders get paid.

This is not a custom transfer tax from 2021. It is a native pons v2 creator tax, rerouted.

---

## Product rules

These are positioning constraints, not optional flavor.

- **3% creator tax.** Fixed at creation. Cannot be raised later.
- **Creator recipient is the splitter contract from block 0.** No founder pocket. No later redirect to an EOA.
- **Buyback off.** A pons buyback cuts the creator share and vests tokens for five years. That breaks “fees hit holders every minute.”
- **Pair is ETH.** Holders receive ETH. That is the product people can feel.
- **Split is pro rata.** Share of supply = share of the tax. No tiers, no boosts, no “loyal holder” multipliers.
- **Cadence is 60 seconds.** The minute is the ritual. Not “when the team claims.”
